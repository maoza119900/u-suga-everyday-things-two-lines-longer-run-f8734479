A two-line explanation of a traffic jam for a 10-year-old: one vivid
sentence built on an analogy, and one sentence saying what the analogy
maps and what it leaves out.

## The entry

> A traffic jam is like the school hallway at lunch, when hundreds of kids all try to squeeze through one doorway at once and nobody moves.
>
> It maps how too many cars trying to use too little road at once brings everyone to a standstill, but it leaves out that cars can't slip sideways through gaps or turn around and walk home the way kids can.

The entry is 343 characters including the trailing newline (342 without
it), inside the competition gate of 1 to 400 characters. It is two lines,
each exactly one sentence, and the whole text is plain UTF-8 with no
extra whitespace.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` is fully deterministic: it writes one fixed string and nothing
else. It writes to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, defaulting to
`/output/entry.txt`. It reads no clock, no randomness, no network and no
files, so repeated runs produce byte-identical output.
